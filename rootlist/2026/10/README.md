## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tarikul-Islam-Anik/Animated-Fluent-Emojis/master/Emojis/Objects/Memo.png" width="22" /> Intelligence Analysis

### Executive Summary

October 2026 has detected **9,810** phishing and scam domains across 19 active days *(month ongoing as of 2026-10-19)*; at 516 domains per day that is a **-5%** change in daily volume from September 2026 (542 per day). The kill rate stands at **33.9%**, with 3,308 domains no longer serving content and 6,447 still live. VirusTotal flagged **94.9%** of the month's domains and Google Safe Browsing flagged 240. The leading registrar was Dynadot Inc. with 4,854 domains (49.5%). MetaMask was the most impersonated brand (763 domains).

### Key Trends

- **Volume** — 9,810 domains in 19 days so far; daily volume -5% vs September 2026.
- **VirusTotal coverage** — 94.9% vs 99.1% the previous month.
- **Registrars** — Dynadot Inc. ranked #1 with 4,854 domains. New in the top 10: Gname.com Pte. Ltd., DYNADOT LLC, Dominet (HK) Limited.
- **Domain zones** — `.shop` led with 4,579 domains (46.7%), followed by `.com`, `.xyz`, `.net`.
- **Brands** — top targets were MetaMask, Google, Apple. New in the top 10: Ethereum, Paypal, Binance.

### Activity Patterns

Detections peaked on **2026-10-07** with 1,995 domains; the lowest day was 2026-10-19 (122). The daily average was 516 domains. Days below 10% of the peak: 4.

Crypto drainer kits identified this month: Solana Drainer (34), Angel Drainer (33), Wallet Connect Abuse (1).

### Registrar Accountability

Dynadot Inc. (4,854, 49.5%), NICENIC INTERNATIONAL GROUP CO., LIMITED (1,264, 12.9%), Spaceship, Inc. (425, 4.3%) were the three registrars with the most phishing domains this month.

Top 3 registrars account for **6,543 domains** (66.7% of threats).

### Detection Landscape

alphaMountain.ai (8,243, 84.0%), Gridinsoft (8,130, 82.9%), Forcepoint ThreatSeeker (6,654, 67.8%), SOCRadar (6,041, 61.6%) were the most active VirusTotal engines. Overall, 9,312 of 9,810 domains (94.9%) had at least one VirusTotal detection.

### Recommendations

- **For Security Teams** — Subscribe to the destroylist feeds and block on VirusTotal consensus; 94.9% of this month's domains are already flagged.
- **For SOC Analysts** — Prioritise domains registered with Dynadot Inc. in the `.shop` zone, the highest-volume combination this month.
- **For Registrars** — Dynadot Inc. and NICENIC INTERNATIONAL GROUP CO., LIMITED hold the largest share of reported domains; faster abuse handling would have the biggest impact.
- **For Brand Owners** — MetaMask, Google, Apple should monitor for look-alike registrations.
- **For End Users** — Check unfamiliar crypto and login links at [api.destroy.tools](https://api.destroy.tools/v1/check?domain=example.com) before connecting a wallet.
