## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tarikul-Islam-Anik/Animated-Fluent-Emojis/master/Emojis/Objects/Memo.png" width="22" /> Intelligence Analysis

### Executive Summary

October 2026 has detected **7,153** phishing and scam domains across 13 active days *(month ongoing as of 2026-10-13)*; at 550 domains per day that is a **+2%** change in daily volume from September 2026 (542 per day). The kill rate stands at **30.5%**, with 2,161 domains no longer serving content and 4,921 still live. VirusTotal flagged **95.1%** of the month's domains and Google Safe Browsing flagged 157. The leading registrar was Dynadot Inc. with 3,213 domains (44.9%). MetaMask was the most impersonated brand (690 domains).

### Key Trends

- **Volume** — 7,153 domains in 13 days so far; daily volume +2% vs September 2026.
- **VirusTotal coverage** — 95.1% vs 99.1% the previous month.
- **Registrars** — Dynadot Inc. ranked #1 with 3,213 domains. New in the top 10: Gname.com Pte. Ltd., DYNADOT LLC, Dominet (HK) Limited.
- **Domain zones** — `.shop` led with 3,259 domains (45.6%), followed by `.com`, `.xyz`, `.net`.
- **Brands** — top targets were MetaMask, Across, Google. New in the top 10: Base, Ethereum, Curve.

### Activity Patterns

Detections peaked on **2026-10-07** with 1,997 domains; the lowest day was 2026-10-05 (172). The daily average was 550 domains. Days below 10% of the peak: 1.

Crypto drainer kits identified this month: Angel Drainer (33), Solana Drainer (14).

### Registrar Accountability

Dynadot Inc. (3,213, 44.9%), NICENIC INTERNATIONAL GROUP CO., LIMITED (1,015, 14.2%), Spaceship, Inc. (210, 2.9%) were the three registrars with the most phishing domains this month.

Top 3 registrars account for **4,438 domains** (62.0% of threats).

### Detection Landscape

Gridinsoft (5,996, 83.8%), alphaMountain.ai (5,992, 83.8%), Forcepoint ThreatSeeker (4,806, 67.2%), SOCRadar (4,324, 60.5%) were the most active VirusTotal engines. Overall, 6,806 of 7,153 domains (95.1%) had at least one VirusTotal detection.

### Recommendations

- **For Security Teams** — Subscribe to the destroylist feeds and block on VirusTotal consensus; 95.1% of this month's domains are already flagged.
- **For SOC Analysts** — Prioritise domains registered with Dynadot Inc. in the `.shop` zone, the highest-volume combination this month.
- **For Registrars** — Dynadot Inc. and NICENIC INTERNATIONAL GROUP CO., LIMITED hold the largest share of reported domains; faster abuse handling would have the biggest impact.
- **For Brand Owners** — MetaMask, Across, Google should monitor for look-alike registrations.
- **For End Users** — Check unfamiliar crypto and login links at [api.destroy.tools](https://api.destroy.tools/v1/check?domain=example.com) before connecting a wallet.
