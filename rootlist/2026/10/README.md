## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tarikul-Islam-Anik/Animated-Fluent-Emojis/master/Emojis/Objects/Memo.png" width="22" /> Intelligence Analysis

### Executive Summary

October 2026 has detected **10,092** phishing and scam domains across 20 active days *(month ongoing as of 2026-10-20)*; at 505 domains per day that is a **-7%** change in daily volume from September 2026 (542 per day). The kill rate stands at **33.3%**, with 3,341 domains no longer serving content and 6,680 still live. VirusTotal flagged **96.1%** of the month's domains and Google Safe Browsing flagged 258. The leading registrar was Dynadot Inc. with 4,924 domains (48.8%). MetaMask was the most impersonated brand (763 domains).

### Key Trends

- **Volume** — 10,092 domains in 20 days so far; daily volume -7% vs September 2026.
- **VirusTotal coverage** — 96.1% vs 99.1% the previous month.
- **Registrars** — Dynadot Inc. ranked #1 with 4,924 domains. New in the top 10: Gname.com Pte. Ltd., DYNADOT LLC, Dominet (HK) Limited.
- **Domain zones** — `.shop` led with 4,599 domains (45.6%), followed by `.com`, `.xyz`, `.net`.
- **Brands** — top targets were MetaMask, Google, Apple. New in the top 10: Ethereum, Paypal, Binance.

### Activity Patterns

Detections peaked on **2026-10-07** with 1,995 domains; the lowest day was 2026-10-20 (10). The daily average was 505 domains. Days below 10% of the peak: 4.

Crypto drainer kits identified this month: Solana Drainer (36), Angel Drainer (33), Wallet Connect Abuse (1).

### Registrar Accountability

Dynadot Inc. (4,924, 48.8%), NICENIC INTERNATIONAL GROUP CO., LIMITED (1,330, 13.2%), Spaceship, Inc. (506, 5.0%) were the three registrars with the most phishing domains this month.

Top 3 registrars account for **6,760 domains** (67.0% of threats).

### Detection Landscape

alphaMountain.ai (8,620, 85.4%), Gridinsoft (8,438, 83.6%), Forcepoint ThreatSeeker (7,063, 70.0%), SOCRadar (6,425, 63.7%) were the most active VirusTotal engines. Overall, 9,701 of 10,092 domains (96.1%) had at least one VirusTotal detection.

### Recommendations

- **For Security Teams** — Subscribe to the destroylist feeds and block on VirusTotal consensus; 96.1% of this month's domains are already flagged.
- **For SOC Analysts** — Prioritise domains registered with Dynadot Inc. in the `.shop` zone, the highest-volume combination this month.
- **For Registrars** — Dynadot Inc. and NICENIC INTERNATIONAL GROUP CO., LIMITED hold the largest share of reported domains; faster abuse handling would have the biggest impact.
- **For Brand Owners** — MetaMask, Google, Apple should monitor for look-alike registrations.
- **For End Users** — Check unfamiliar crypto and login links at [api.destroy.tools](https://api.destroy.tools/v1/check?domain=example.com) before connecting a wallet.
