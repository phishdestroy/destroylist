## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tarikul-Islam-Anik/Animated-Fluent-Emojis/master/Emojis/Objects/Memo.png" width="22" /> Intelligence Analysis

### Executive Summary

October 2026 has detected **14,145** phishing and scam domains across 24 active days *(month ongoing as of 2026-10-24)*; at 589 domains per day that is a **+9%** change in daily volume from September 2026 (542 per day). The kill rate stands at **33.1%**, with 4,646 domains no longer serving content and 9,373 still live. VirusTotal flagged **84.6%** of the month's domains and Google Safe Browsing flagged 581. The leading registrar was Dynadot Inc. with 5,852 domains (41.4%). MetaMask was the most impersonated brand (778 domains).

### Key Trends

- **Volume** — 14,145 domains in 24 days so far; daily volume +9% vs September 2026.
- **VirusTotal coverage** — 84.6% vs 99.1% the previous month.
- **Registrars** — Dynadot Inc. ranked #1 with 5,852 domains. New in the top 10: Cloudflare, Inc., Vercel Inc., GitHub, Inc..
- **Domain zones** — `.shop` led with 4,995 domains (35.3%), followed by `.com`, `.app`, `.dev`.
- **Brands** — top targets were MetaMask, Ledger, Google. New in the top 10: Ethereum, Celer, Binance.

### Activity Patterns

Detections peaked on **2026-10-07** with 1,995 domains; the lowest day was 2026-10-14 (159). The daily average was 589 domains. Days below 10% of the peak: 4.

Crypto drainer kits identified this month: Solana Drainer (36), Angel Drainer (33), wallet_drainer (4), Wallet Connect Abuse (1).

### Registrar Accountability

Dynadot Inc. (5,852, 41.4%), NICENIC INTERNATIONAL GROUP CO., LIMITED (1,655, 11.7%), Cloudflare, Inc. (800, 5.7%) were the three registrars with the most phishing domains this month.

Top 3 registrars account for **8,307 domains** (58.7% of threats).

### Detection Landscape

alphaMountain.ai (10,748, 76.0%), Gridinsoft (10,408, 73.6%), Forcepoint ThreatSeeker (9,273, 65.6%), SOCRadar (8,363, 59.1%) were the most active VirusTotal engines. Overall, 11,964 of 14,145 domains (84.6%) had at least one VirusTotal detection.

### Recommendations

- **For Security Teams** — Subscribe to the destroylist feeds and block on VirusTotal consensus; 84.6% of this month's domains are already flagged.
- **For SOC Analysts** — Prioritise domains registered with Dynadot Inc. in the `.shop` zone, the highest-volume combination this month.
- **For Registrars** — Dynadot Inc. and NICENIC INTERNATIONAL GROUP CO., LIMITED hold the largest share of reported domains; faster abuse handling would have the biggest impact.
- **For Brand Owners** — MetaMask, Ledger, Google should monitor for look-alike registrations.
- **For End Users** — Check unfamiliar crypto and login links at [api.destroy.tools](https://api.destroy.tools/v1/check?domain=example.com) before connecting a wallet.
