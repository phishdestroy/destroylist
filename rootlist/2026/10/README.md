## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tarikul-Islam-Anik/Animated-Fluent-Emojis/master/Emojis/Objects/Memo.png" width="22" /> Intelligence Analysis

### Executive Summary

October 2026 has detected **15,027** phishing and scam domains across 25 active days *(month ongoing as of 2026-10-25)*; at 601 domains per day that is a **+11%** change in daily volume from September 2026 (542 per day). The kill rate stands at **35.8%**, with 5,104 domains no longer serving content and 9,152 still live. VirusTotal flagged **93.0%** of the month's domains and Google Safe Browsing flagged 619. The leading registrar was Dynadot Inc. with 6,178 domains (41.1%). MetaMask was the most impersonated brand (779 domains).

### Key Trends

- **Volume** — 15,027 domains in 25 days so far; daily volume +11% vs September 2026.
- **VirusTotal coverage** — 93.0% vs 99.1% the previous month.
- **Registrars** — Dynadot Inc. ranked #1 with 6,178 domains. New in the top 10: Cloudflare, Inc., Vercel Inc., GitHub, Inc..
- **Domain zones** — `.shop` led with 5,117 domains (34.1%), followed by `.com`, `.app`, `.dev`.
- **Brands** — top targets were MetaMask, Paypal, Ledger. New in the top 10: Paypal, Ethereum, Celer.

### Activity Patterns

Detections peaked on **2026-10-07** with 1,995 domains; the lowest day was 2026-10-14 (159). The daily average was 601 domains. Days below 10% of the peak: 3.

Crypto drainer kits identified this month: Solana Drainer (36), Angel Drainer (33), wallet_drainer (4), Wallet Connect Abuse (1).

### Registrar Accountability

Dynadot Inc. (6,178, 41.1%), NICENIC INTERNATIONAL GROUP CO., LIMITED (1,680, 11.2%), Cloudflare, Inc. (826, 5.5%) were the three registrars with the most phishing domains this month.

Top 3 registrars account for **8,684 domains** (57.8% of threats).

### Detection Landscape

alphaMountain.ai (12,522, 83.3%), Gridinsoft (11,698, 77.8%), Forcepoint ThreatSeeker (10,494, 69.8%), SOCRadar (9,948, 66.2%) were the most active VirusTotal engines. Overall, 13,968 of 15,027 domains (93.0%) had at least one VirusTotal detection.

### Recommendations

- **For Security Teams** — Subscribe to the destroylist feeds and block on VirusTotal consensus; 93.0% of this month's domains are already flagged.
- **For SOC Analysts** — Prioritise domains registered with Dynadot Inc. in the `.shop` zone, the highest-volume combination this month.
- **For Registrars** — Dynadot Inc. and NICENIC INTERNATIONAL GROUP CO., LIMITED hold the largest share of reported domains; faster abuse handling would have the biggest impact.
- **For Brand Owners** — MetaMask, Paypal, Ledger should monitor for look-alike registrations.
- **For End Users** — Check unfamiliar crypto and login links at [api.destroy.tools](https://api.destroy.tools/v1/check?domain=example.com) before connecting a wallet.
