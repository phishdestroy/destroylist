## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tarikul-Islam-Anik/Animated-Fluent-Emojis/master/Emojis/Objects/Memo.png" width="22" /> Intelligence Analysis

### Executive Summary

October 2026 has detected **8,046** phishing and scam domains across 15 active days *(month ongoing as of 2026-10-15)*; at 536 domains per day that is a **-1%** change in daily volume from September 2026 (542 per day). The kill rate stands at **33.6%**, with 2,665 domains no longer serving content and 5,255 still live. VirusTotal flagged **92.7%** of the month's domains and Google Safe Browsing flagged 181. The leading registrar was Dynadot Inc. with 3,938 domains (48.9%). MetaMask was the most impersonated brand (729 domains).

### Key Trends

- **Volume** — 8,046 domains in 15 days so far; daily volume -1% vs September 2026.
- **VirusTotal coverage** — 92.7% vs 99.1% the previous month.
- **Registrars** — Dynadot Inc. ranked #1 with 3,938 domains. New in the top 10: Gname.com Pte. Ltd., DYNADOT LLC, Dominet (HK) Limited.
- **Domain zones** — `.shop` led with 3,561 domains (44.3%), followed by `.com`, `.xyz`, `.net`.
- **Brands** — top targets were MetaMask, Across, Google. New in the top 10: Base, Ethereum, Curve.

### Activity Patterns

Detections peaked on **2026-10-07** with 1,997 domains; the lowest day was 2026-10-15 (81). The daily average was 536 domains. Days below 10% of the peak: 3.

Crypto drainer kits identified this month: Angel Drainer (33), Solana Drainer (30), Wallet Connect Abuse (1).

### Registrar Accountability

Dynadot Inc. (3,938, 48.9%), NICENIC INTERNATIONAL GROUP CO., LIMITED (1,203, 15.0%), Spaceship, Inc. (255, 3.2%) were the three registrars with the most phishing domains this month.

Top 3 registrars account for **5,396 domains** (67.1% of threats).

### Detection Landscape

alphaMountain.ai (6,515, 81.0%), Gridinsoft (6,472, 80.4%), Forcepoint ThreatSeeker (5,091, 63.3%), SOCRadar (4,532, 56.3%) were the most active VirusTotal engines. Overall, 7,455 of 8,046 domains (92.7%) had at least one VirusTotal detection.

### Recommendations

- **For Security Teams** — Subscribe to the destroylist feeds and block on VirusTotal consensus; 92.7% of this month's domains are already flagged.
- **For SOC Analysts** — Prioritise domains registered with Dynadot Inc. in the `.shop` zone, the highest-volume combination this month.
- **For Registrars** — Dynadot Inc. and NICENIC INTERNATIONAL GROUP CO., LIMITED hold the largest share of reported domains; faster abuse handling would have the biggest impact.
- **For Brand Owners** — MetaMask, Across, Google should monitor for look-alike registrations.
- **For End Users** — Check unfamiliar crypto and login links at [api.destroy.tools](https://api.destroy.tools/v1/check?domain=example.com) before connecting a wallet.
