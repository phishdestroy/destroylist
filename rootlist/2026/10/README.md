## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tarikul-Islam-Anik/Animated-Fluent-Emojis/master/Emojis/Objects/Memo.png" width="22" /> Intelligence Analysis

### Executive Summary

October 2026 detected **19,587** phishing and scam domains across 31 active days , a **+21%** change from September 2026's 16,250. The kill rate stands at **56.4%**, with 10,821 domains no longer serving content and 8,350 still live. VirusTotal flagged **96.9%** of the month's domains and Google Safe Browsing flagged 892. The leading registrar was Dynadot Inc. with 7,302 domains (37.3%). MetaMask was the most impersonated brand (815 domains).

### Key Trends

- **Volume** — 19,587 domains vs 16,250 in September 2026 (+21%).
- **VirusTotal coverage** — 96.9% vs 99.1% the previous month.
- **Registrars** — Dynadot Inc. ranked #1 with 7,302 domains. New in the top 10: Cloudflare, Inc., Google LLC, Vercel Inc..
- **Domain zones** — `.shop` led with 5,605 domains (28.6%), followed by `.com`, `.app`, `.dev`.
- **Brands** — top targets were MetaMask, Ledger, Paypal. New in the top 10: Paypal, Ethereum, Celer.

### Activity Patterns

Detections peaked on **2026-10-07** with 1,994 domains; the lowest day was 2026-10-14 (159). The daily average was 632 domains. Days below 10% of the peak: 3.

Crypto drainer kits identified this month: Solana Drainer (36), Angel Drainer (33), wallet_drainer (6), Wallet Connect Abuse (1).

### Registrar Accountability

Dynadot Inc. (7,302, 37.3%), NICENIC INTERNATIONAL GROUP CO., LIMITED (2,127, 10.9%), Cloudflare, Inc. (1,040, 5.3%) were the three registrars with the most phishing domains this month.

Top 3 registrars account for **10,469 domains** (53.4% of threats).

### Detection Landscape

alphaMountain.ai (17,249, 88.1%), Gridinsoft (16,050, 81.9%), Forcepoint ThreatSeeker (15,008, 76.6%), SOCRadar (14,785, 75.5%) were the most active VirusTotal engines. Overall, 18,985 of 19,587 domains (96.9%) had at least one VirusTotal detection.

### Recommendations

- **For Security Teams** — Subscribe to the destroylist feeds and block on VirusTotal consensus; 96.9% of this month's domains are already flagged.
- **For SOC Analysts** — Prioritise domains registered with Dynadot Inc. in the `.shop` zone, the highest-volume combination this month.
- **For Registrars** — Dynadot Inc. and NICENIC INTERNATIONAL GROUP CO., LIMITED hold the largest share of reported domains; faster abuse handling would have the biggest impact.
- **For Brand Owners** — MetaMask, Ledger, Paypal should monitor for look-alike registrations.
- **For End Users** — Check unfamiliar crypto and login links at [api.destroy.tools](https://api.destroy.tools/v1/check?domain=example.com) before connecting a wallet.
