## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tarikul-Islam-Anik/Animated-Fluent-Emojis/master/Emojis/Objects/Memo.png" width="22" /> Intelligence Analysis

### Executive Summary
In December 2025, PhishDestroy identified a total of 11,773 phishing domains, marking a 6.4% decrease from November's 12,581. Of these, 7,080 domains remained live, resulting in a kill rate of 39.9%. The most significant finding was a notable concentration of phishing activity in the USA, accounting for 5,443 domains. Additionally, the average response time for threat mitigation improved to 1,434.4 hours, indicating a decrease in reaction time from the previous month.

### Key Trends
- **Crypto/Drainer Landscape** — Angel Drainer was the most prevalent, responsible for 746 incidents. Wallet Connect Abuse followed with 418 cases, showing a slight increase from last month.
- **Brand Targeting Shifts** — Crypto Scam brands topped the list with 822 targeted domains, a shift from last month's focus on Coinbase, which saw a reduction to 716 domains.
- **Geographic Hotspots** — The USA led with 5,443 domains, while Hong Kong saw an uptick to 1,526 domains, reflecting a geographic expansion of phishing activities.
- **TLD Abuse Patterns** — The .com TLD was abused in 3,816 cases, maintaining its lead. The .app TLD saw a decrease to 991 domains, while .xyz remained stable at 483.
- **Registrar Abuse Concentration** — NICENIC INTERNATIONAL GROUP CO., LIMITED accounted for 1,270 domains, surpassing Cloudflare, Inc., which registered 1,100 domains.
- **Detection/Response Metrics** — 11,557 domains were flagged by VT vendors, slightly lower than last month, with Google Safe Browsing identifying 1,963 domains, a minor increase.

### Threat Actor Tactics
Threat actors continued to exploit infrastructure and hosting services through fast-flux networks and bulletproof hosting, complicating takedown efforts. Nameserver clustering was particularly prominent in the USA and Hong Kong, facilitating rapid domain switching to evade detection.

Drainer and phishing kit evolution saw increased sophistication, with Angel Drainer and Wallet Connect Abuse kits showing advanced techniques for wallet targeting. The emergence of Solana Drainer and the rare use of Ice Phishing kits underscore the diversification of attack vectors.

Evasion and obfuscation tactics revealed that the VT vendor detection spread remained robust, with SOCRadar and CyRadar leading in detections. However, gaps persist, particularly in engines like BitDefender, allowing threat actors to bypass specific detection mechanisms through obfuscation and rapid domain shifts.

### Registrar Accountability
The top five registrars facilitating phishing domains were NICENIC INTERNATIONAL GROUP CO., LIMITED (1,270 domains, 10.8%), Cloudflare, Inc. (1,100, 9.3%), PDR Ltd. (554, 4.7%), CSC Corporate Domains, Inc. (547, 4.6%), and Gname.com Pte. Ltd. (514, 4.4%). These registrars accounted for a significant portion of the phishing landscape.

Notably, Cloudflare, Inc. showed improvement by reducing the number of abusive domains compared to last month. NICENIC INTERNATIONAL GROUP CO., LIMITED, however, saw an increase in abuse. CSC Corporate Domains, Inc. and Gname.com Pte. Ltd. are new entrants in the top five, indicating shifting registrar dynamics.

### Detection Landscape
SOCRadar led the VT vendor data with 8,774 detections, followed closely by CyRadar at 8,317. Fortinet and alphaMountain.ai also showed strong performance, highlighting their proactive threat detection capabilities. Despite these strengths, detection gaps remain, particularly with lower-performing engines like BitDefender, emphasizing the need for comprehensive multi-engine approaches for defenders.

### Recommendations
- **For Security Teams** — Implement multi-layered security solutions to address gaps in single-engine detections and enhance phishing domain identification.
- **For SOC Analysts** — Prioritize monitoring of fast-flux networks and bulletproof hosting services, focusing on rapid domain switching tactics.
- **For Registrars** — Strengthen domain registration vetting processes to reduce the registration of malicious domains, particularly in high-abuse TLDs.
- **For Browser Vendors** — Enhance real-time phishing detection capabilities within browsers, focusing on .com and .app TLDs, which are heavily abused.
- **For Crypto Projects** — Increase user awareness and implement robust anti-phishing measures, particularly against Angel Drainer and Wallet Connect Abuse.
- **For End Users** — Stay vigilant against phishing attempts, regularly update security software, and verify the authenticity of crypto-related communications.
