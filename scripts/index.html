BEFORE YOU INTEGRATE

Clear expectations.

Does an unlisted domain mean it is safe?

No. New threats, dormant domains and cloaked content may not be detected. Use these feeds alongside other evidence and your own controls.

Why are API counts different from the feed counts?

The page shows published feed entries. The API normalizes and deduplicates domains, including URL and www variants. Lists overlap, so adding their counts does not produce a unique-domain total.

How do I choose between full, live and content feeds?

Full feeds retain broader coverage. Live feeds contain entries resolving in DNS at the last check. Content feeds pass additional HTTP checks. None of these is a guarantee that every malicious site is present.

Can I pay to remove a domain?

No. There are no paid delistings. Use the free official appeal route for an evidence-based review.

How is the project licensed?

The DestroyList repository declares the MIT license. Upstream source data may carry separate conditions; inspect each source before redistributing it.
