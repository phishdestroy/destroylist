What do these counts represent?

Monthly totals come from each report’s meta.total. The annual total is their sum, not an independently deduplicated count of unique domains. Changes compare adjacent report totals, not changes in worldwide phishing.

Are live and dead statuses current?

No. These are statuses recorded in the generated historical reports. “Dead” is not evidence of a confirmed takedown or attribution to this project. This page does not perform live checks.

Why are some months or dates sparse?

Archive coverage varies. Early months have very few entries; December’s provided daily series ends on December 6. Missing days are shown as unavailable rather than zero. Compare collection periods with care.

How are detection and infrastructure figures used?

VirusTotal and Google Safe Browsing figures are report snapshots. Missing detections do not establish safety. Registrar and domain-zone rankings describe this dataset and do not imply the provider participated in abuse. Top lists may omit other or unknown records.

Can I reproduce the results?

Each source link points to the exact repository revision used here. Download the annual CSV or open any month’s source JSON to inspect the figures. Only trailing commas outside strings were removed to parse the supplied report JSON; numeric values were preserved.
