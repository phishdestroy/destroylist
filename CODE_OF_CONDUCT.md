## Our Pledge

We're building a tool that protects real people from fraud. Everyone who contributes — whether reporting a domain, opening a PR, or just using the list — is part of that.

We keep this space respectful and focused on the work.

---

## What We Expect

**Do:**
- Be direct and constructive
- Accept feedback without drama
- Assume good faith until proven otherwise
- Stay on topic — this project is about fighting phishing

**Don't:**
- Harass, insult, or attack contributors
- Submit fake reports, abuse GitHub issues, or file false DMCA claims
- Share private information without consent
- Use this project's community channels for anything unrelated to its purpose

This applies to GitHub issues, PRs, discussions, and any other channel connected to this project.

---

## Enforcement

Report violations to **conduct@phishdestroy.io** — reviewed promptly and confidentially.

Operators of blocked domains attempting to abuse this project's community channels will be banned and reported.
