### Executive Summary
In January 2026, PhishDestroy identified 8,932 phishing domains, marking a 24.1% decrease from December 2025's total of 11,773. Of these, 4,254 domains were live, while 4,678 were inactive, resulting in a kill rate of 52.4%. VirusTotal flagged 8,896 domains, highlighting effective detection. The most significant finding this month is the notable reduction in phishing domains, coupled with an improved average response time of 759.8 hours, indicating enhanced mitigation efforts.

### Key Trends
- **Crypto/Drainer Landscape** — Solana drainer tactics remained prevalent with 213 incidents, although down from 342 last month, reflecting a 37.7% decrease.
- **Brand Targeting Shifts** — Crypto Scam brands were the most targeted with 793 incidents, a slight increase from 758, indicating continued interest in cryptocurrency vulnerabilities.
- **Geographic Hotspots** — The USA remains the top host with 2,715 incidents, a decrease from 3,201, showing a 15.2% reduction, while Hong Kong saw a slight increase to 801 from 789.
- **TLD Abuse Patterns** — The '.com' TLD was abused 3,249 times, down from 4,112, showing a 21% decrease, maintaining its lead in abuse frequency.
- **Registrar Abuse Concentration** — NICENIC INTERNATIONAL GROUP CO., LIMITED led with 1,304 domains, a minor decrease from 1,362.
- **Detection/Response Metrics** — The average response time improved to 759.8 hours from 842.3 hours, indicating faster intervention and takedown processes.

### Threat Actor Tactics
Threat actors continue to leverage robust infrastructure and hosting solutions, with noticeable patterns of fast-flux techniques and bulletproof hosting services. CDN abuse is prevalent, facilitating rapid deployment and takedown evasion. Nameserver clustering remains a favored tactic, complicating attribution and mitigation efforts.

Drainer and phishing kit evolution reveal increasing sophistication, with a notable presence of the "solana_drainer" and "Angel Drainer" kits. These kits exhibit enhanced techniques targeting Solana wallets, leveraging wallet connect abuse mechanisms to siphon funds stealthily.

Evasion and obfuscation techniques show a tactical adaptation to avoid detection by popular antivirus engines. VirusTotal data indicates gaps in coverage, particularly with engines that lack heuristic analysis capabilities, allowing threat actors to bypass specific detection engines effectively.

### Registrar Accountability
The top five registrars for phishing domain registrations were NICENIC INTERNATIONAL GROUP CO., LIMITED (1,304 domains, 14.6%), PDR Ltd. (942 domains, 10.5%), Gname.com (771 domains, 8.6%), NameSilo, LLC (423 domains, 4.7%), and Dynadot Inc (388 domains, 4.3%). These registrars collectively accounted for a significant share of phishing domain registrations, highlighting areas for increased scrutiny and intervention.

Improvements were noted with PDR Ltd., showing a reduction in abusive registrations, while NICENIC INTERNATIONAL GROUP CO., LIMITED saw a slight decrease. Gname.com and NameSilo, LLC experienced increases, suggesting a need for enhanced monitoring. Dynadot Inc is a new entrant to the top five, indicating a shift in threat actor preferences.

### Detection Landscape
VirusTotal data reveals SOCRadar as the leading engine, flagging 8,120 domains, followed by Gridinsoft (6,035) and Fortinet (5,759). Detection gaps persist, particularly among engines with limited threat intelligence integration. These gaps underscore the need for continuous improvement in detection capabilities to preemptively identify and mitigate emerging threats.

### Recommendations
- **For Security Teams** — Implement advanced threat intelligence feeds to enhance early detection and response to phishing domains.
- **For SOC Analysts** — Prioritize analysis of domains from registrars with high abuse concentrations for proactive threat hunting.
- **For Registrars** — Strengthen verification processes to reduce the registration of phishing domains and collaborate with cybersecurity firms for threat intelligence sharing.
- **For Browser Vendors** — Integrate real-time phishing domain blacklists to improve user protection against fraudulent sites.
- **For Crypto Projects** — Educate users on secure wallet practices and the risks posed by phishing kits targeting crypto assets.
- **For End Users** — Stay informed about phishing tactics and verify the authenticity of sites before entering sensitive information.
