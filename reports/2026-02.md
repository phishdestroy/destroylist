## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tarikul-Islam-Anik/Animated-Fluent-Emojis/master/Emojis/Objects/Memo.png" width="22" /> Intelligence Analysis

### Executive Summary
In February 2026, PhishDestroy detected a total of 18,207 phishing domains, marking a significant increase of 103.8% from the previous month's 8,932. Of these, 12,754 domains were active, while 3,456 were neutralized, resulting in a kill rate of 19.0%. The most significant finding was the high concentration of phishing domains registered through NICENIC INTERNATIONAL GROUP CO., LIMITED, which accounted for 7,471 domains. Additionally, the average response time for takedown actions was 273.1 hours.

### Key Trends
- **Crypto/Drainer Landscape** — The "solana_drainer" was the most prevalent, with 355 instances, indicating a focused attack vector on Solana-based wallets.
- **Brand Targeting Shifts** — Facebook Pixel was targeted 778 times, a notable shift towards exploiting analytics and tracking tools.
- **Geographic Hotspots** — The USA remained the top hotspot, with 3,956 domains, while Hong Kong saw a rise to 2,353 domains.
- **TLD Abuse Patterns** — The ".com" TLD was abused the most with 6,427 domains, a 68.5% increase from January.
- **Registrar Abuse Concentration** — NICENIC INTERNATIONAL GROUP CO., LIMITED led with a 41.0% share of detected domains, a significant concentration.
- **Detection/Response Metrics** — VirusTotal flagged 17,987 domains, with a 1.4% increase in overall detection accuracy from January.

### Threat Actor Tactics
Threat actors continued to leverage fast-flux hosting and nameserver clustering to obfuscate the source of phishing attacks. Bulletproof hosting services were less prevalent this month, indicating a possible shift towards more transient infrastructure setups.

Drainer kits showed increased sophistication, particularly targeting Solana and other crypto wallets. The "solana_drainer" accounted for the majority of drainer activity, demonstrating a focused attack on Solana's ecosystem. Additionally, new variants of wallet connect abuse were identified, albeit in smaller numbers.

Evasion techniques included advanced obfuscation methods, with gaps in detection primarily from engines not specializing in fast-flux analysis. Notably, the VT vendor detection spread indicated that while SOCRadar led with 15,397 detections, there remains a gap in comprehensive coverage, allowing some actors to evade detection by less vigilant engines.

### Registrar Accountability
NICENIC INTERNATIONAL GROUP CO., LIMITED was the leading registrar with 7,471 domains, comprising 41.0% of all detected phishing sites. Other notable registrars include PDR Ltd. (787 domains) and Cloudflare, Inc. (744 domains). These figures highlight the need for increased scrutiny and accountability among registrars.

While NICENIC INTERNATIONAL GROUP CO., LIMITED's numbers worsened, PDR Ltd. showed a slight improvement in controlling phishing activities. Cloudflare, Inc. emerged as a new entrant in the top five, emphasizing the shifting landscape of registrar accountability.

### Detection Landscape
SOCRadar led the VT vendor data with 15,397 detections, followed by Fortinet at 11,281. Detection gaps remain, particularly in engines not optimized for rapid domain flux. This highlights the need for continuous improvement in detection technologies to keep pace with evolving threat tactics.

### Recommendations
- **For Security Teams** — Implement continuous monitoring and rapid response protocols to manage the increased phishing domain activity efficiently.
- **For SOC Analysts** — Focus on identifying and mitigating threats from fast-flux and nameserver clustering techniques.
- **For Registrars** — Enhance verification processes to prevent the registration of malicious domains, particularly in high-abuse TLDs.
- **For Browser Vendors** — Integrate advanced phishing site detection mechanisms directly into browsers to protect end-users.
- **For Crypto Projects** — Collaborate with security firms to secure wallet infrastructures against drainer kits targeting specific ecosystems like Solana.
- **For End Users** — Stay informed about the latest phishing tactics and ensure browsers and anti-virus software are up to date to mitigate risks.
