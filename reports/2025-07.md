## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tarikul-Islam-Anik/Animated-Fluent-Emojis/master/Emojis/Objects/Memo.png" width="22" /> Intelligence Analysis

### Executive Summary
In July 2025, PhishDestroy identified a total of 700 phishing domains, representing an unprecedented surge of 17,400.0% compared to the mere 4 domains detected in the previous month. Of these, 182 domains were still active, while 518 had been neutralized, achieving a kill rate of 74.0%. Notably, 656 domains were flagged by VirusTotal (VT), while Google Safe Browsing (GSB) identified only 19. The average response time was 4981.9 hours, indicating a significant delay in domain takedown efforts.

### Key Trends
- **Crypto/Drainer Landscape** — Angel Drainer dominated the landscape with 183 instances, far outpacing other drainers like Wallet Connect Abuse (11) and new entries like Solana Drainer (1).
- **Brand Targeting Shifts** — Generic Crypto brands were targeted 29 times, with SushiSwap experiencing 20 incidents, marking a shift from traditional financial institutions to decentralized finance platforms.
- **Geographic Hotspots** — The USA remained the leading source with 295 domains, followed by Malaysia (85), indicating a geographical concentration in North America and Southeast Asia.
- **TLD Abuse Patterns** — The .com TLD was the most abused with 304 domains, significantly outnumbering .xyz (84) and .org (27), showing a preference for more trusted TLDs.
- **Registrar Abuse Concentration** — NameSilo, LLC was the top registrar with 75 domains, closely followed by PDR Ltd. with 71, reflecting a concentration of abuse in these registrars.
- **Detection/Response Metrics** — 656 domains were flagged by VT, yet only 19 by GSB, highlighting a gap in real-time detection capabilities.

### Threat Actor Tactics
Threat actors continued to exploit fast-flux hosting and bulletproof hosting services to maintain domain resilience. Nameserver clustering was observed, indicating coordinated efforts to obscure domain origins and enhance longevity.

The evolution of drainer kits was evident, with Angel Drainer showcasing advanced techniques such as multi-chain wallet targeting and adaptive phishing pages, increasing their effectiveness against less vigilant users. Additionally, new drainers like Solana Drainer were identified, reflecting the diversification in targeting strategies.

Evasion tactics were sophisticated, with actors employing advanced obfuscation techniques to bypass detection. The VT vendor detection spread highlighted significant gaps, as major engines like GSB underperformed. Actors showed adeptness in avoiding detection from specific engines by rapidly changing domain attributes and leveraging less scrutinized registrars.

### Registrar Accountability
The top five registrars facilitating phishing domains were NameSilo, LLC (75), PDR Ltd. (71), HOSTINGER operations, UAB (42), NICENIC INTERNATIONAL GROUP CO., LIMITED (41), and Web Commerce Communications Limited dba WebNic.cc (40). These registrars accounted for a significant share of the phishing domain landscape, suggesting a need for improved verification processes.

While NameSilo and PDR Ltd. showed increased domain abuse, HOSTINGER operations displayed a slight improvement in domain management. NICENIC INTERNATIONAL GROUP and WebNic.cc emerged as new entrants in the top five, warranting closer scrutiny of their registration practices.

### Detection Landscape
VirusTotal data indicated that alphaMountain.ai led with 360 detections, followed by Fortinet with 294. However, the disparity between VT and GSB detections underscores a critical gap in real-time threat identification. This suggests that defenders must diversify their detection sources to enhance coverage and response times.

### Recommendations
- **For Security Teams** — Integrate multiple threat intelligence feeds to ensure comprehensive coverage and faster response to phishing threats.
- **For SOC Analysts** — Prioritize monitoring of .com and .xyz TLDs, as they are the most commonly abused, to improve detection accuracy.
- **For Registrars** — Implement stricter domain registration checks and enhance monitoring for suspicious activities to reduce abuse.
- **For Browser Vendors** — Collaborate with threat intelligence providers to improve real-time detection and blocking of phishing domains.
- **For Crypto Projects** — Educate users on secure wallet practices and implement additional security layers to protect against drainer attacks.
- **For End Users** — Remain vigilant and verify URLs before entering sensitive information, especially when prompted by unsolicited communications.
